# Kiki's Delivery Service v1.0.0: a painted town, drawn on twos

An unofficial fan game: a third-person delivery adventure through the seaside town of Koriko, made to look and move like a hand-painted animated film. **Watch the trailer:** [Kikis-Delivery-Service-trailer.mp4](https://github.com/gazhenko/kikis-delivery-service/releases/download/v1.0.0/Kikis-Delivery-Service-trailer.mp4).

## The film look
- **Painted backgrounds.** The town is shaded in three flat washes (sunlit, half-tone and shadow) whose edges wobble with the brush marks of the painted atlases, with hard-edged cast shadows that take the sky's blue. A full-screen painter's line traces silhouettes and creases in warm ink that thins with distance, and a film grade warms the daylight and lifts the blacks.
- **Cel characters.** Kiki and Jiji are painted in the film's palette with one hard shadow shape per paint, a lit face, and colour-matched trace lines around every part.
- **Drawn on twos.** Kiki's pose is exposed twelve times a second while the camera and the town move smoothly, and she goes on ones for fast actions: a push-off, a hard landing, a brake, a smear. Hair, bow, hem, satchel and Jiji follow on the same drawing clock. Her planted foot stays exact every frame, so nothing slides.
- **The film's acting.** The seated flight with the smock over her lap, the walk with weight over the standing leg and the free arm opposing the legs, a bow and a wave when a parcel is handed over, blinks and glances, and Jiji's own ear flicks and tail.

## The game
- Six delivery courts across the bakery street, the clock square, the harbour, Madame's garden, Tombo's workshop and the airship; timed jobs with tips; fragile, rush, heavy, night and airship freight that each need the right broom.
- Osono's bakery: shopping, six recipes with buffs, six broom upgrades, hunger and energy, sleep, and hospital returns.
- Walking, mounting, flight with cruise, boost and braking, assisted landings on courts and clear streets.
- A synthesized soundscape and an original waltz; every sound is generated by the game.
- Keyboard and mouse or an Xbox or PlayStation controller, with camera and sound settings. Cozy or Challenging pace. Local saves.

## Platforms
macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel), Linux x64 and Windows x64. The macOS and Linux players were verified natively with the game's automated checks; the Windows player was built from the same source but has not been run on Windows yet.
